The actual PSID program/data bytes are bundled in launcher/sid-tracks.js using
base64 for direct-from-disk loading. They are not commercial MP3 recordings,
imitations, or rendered whole-track PCM. Playback executes the original tune
programs through a local 6510/SID emulator.
